## 1. Research Findings — Tuileries Patisserie (tuileriespatisserie.in)

### Design Language — PREMIUM GOLD/BLACK
- **Color scheme:** Dark backgrounds (#1a1a1a), gold accents, white text — luxurious feel
- **Typography:** Serif fonts for headings (elegant, French-inspired), clean sans-serif for body
- **Photography:** Full-width hero images, professional food photography, dark moody backgrounds
- **Layout:** Clean whitespace, generous padding, no clutter
- **Platform:** Shopify-based (Shopify storefront)

### Key Sections (Homepage)
1. **Announcement bar** — scrolling text: "4.8 Rating | 5000+ Orders Delivered • Same Day Delivery • Egg Free Options • Elegant Designer Cake Finishes"
2. **Hero carousel** — full-width stunning cake photos with overlay text
3. **Summer Collection** — horizontal product slider
4. **Under ₹1500 Collection** — product grid
5. **"Your Celebration Starts with the Perfect Cake"** — category cards (1kg, Half kg, Eggless, Dry Cakes, Cheesecake)
6. **Shop By Occasions** — Birthday, Wedding, Anniversary, Gift Hampers (image cards)
7. **"Find the Perfect Cake"** — another product slider
8. **Gift Hampers** section
9. **Promo banner** — "10% OFF Above ₹3499"
10. **About section** with YouTube video embed
11. **Testimonials carousel** — customer photos + reviews
12. **SEO text block** — detailed descriptions
13. **"Get Your Cake Delivered Today"** CTA → WhatsApp
14. **Blog/News** section
15. **FAQ accordion**
16. **Instagram feed** grid
17. **Footer** — About, Shop Categories, Policies, Newsletter

### Product Cards (Tuileries Style)
- **Image:** Clean product photo on solid/white background, multiple angles on hover
- **Name:** Below image, clean serif font
- **Price:** "Regular price ₹X,XXX.00" format
- **Badge:** "Out of Stock" shown on unavailable items
- **Hover:** Second image appears (image swap animation)
- **No ADD button visible** — click card → product page → add to cart

### Features We're Missing
- ✨ Announcement bar (scrolling trust signals)
- ✨ Shop by Occasion (Birthday, Wedding, Anniversary)
- ✨ Testimonials section with customer photos
- ✨ Instagram feed integration
- ✨ Blog/news section
- ✨ FAQ accordion
- ✨ YouTube video embed
- ✨ Newsletter signup
- ✨ Wishlist functionality
- ✨ Product image swap on hover
- ✨ WhatsApp floating button
- ✨ Cookie consent banner

## 5. Redesign Plan — Premium Golden Pink Theme

### New Color Palette (Tuileries-inspired + Bliss Bakery pink)

```
Primary:     #C8A27C (warm gold)
Primary Alt: #D4A0A0 (rose pink — keep from logo)
Background:  #FDF8F3 (warm cream)
Surface:     #FFFFFF
Text:        #1A1A1A (near black)
Text Muted:  #6B5E50 (warm gray)
Accent:      #8B6E4E (bronze)
Success:     #2D7A4F (forest green)
Border:      #E8DFD4 (warm border)
Dark BG:     #1E1916 (for sections like footer, testimonials)
```

### Typography
- **Headings:** `Playfair Display` or `Cormorant Garamond` (serif, elegant)
- **Body:** `Inter` or `DM Sans` (clean, modern sans-serif)
- **Accent:** Current Geist works for UI elements

### Redesign Sections (New Homepage Layout)

```
1. ANNOUNCEMENT BAR (scrolling)
   "100% Eggless & Vegetarian 🌿 | Same Day Pickup Available 🕐 | Premium Quality Ingredients ✨"

2. HEADER (transparent on hero, solid on scroll)
   Logo | Nav (Menu, Custom Cakes, Occasions, Offers, About) | Search | Cart | Account

3. HERO (full-width image/video carousel)
   Stunning cake photography with elegant overlay text
   "Artisan Eggless Cakes, Crafted with Love"
   [Order Now] [Custom Cake]

4. TRUST BADGES BAR
   🌿 100% Vegetarian | 🥚✗ Eggless | ⏰ Same Day Pickup | ⭐ Premium Quality

5. BESTSELLERS (horizontal slider — Swiper.js style)
   Premium product cards with image, name, price, quick-add

6. SHOP BY OCCASION (image cards)
   Birthday | Anniversary | Wedding | Festival | Corporate

7. SHOP BY CATEGORY (elegant grid)
   Cakes | Pastries | Brownies | Cookies | Breads | Custom Cakes

8. FEATURED COLLECTION (product grid with filters)

9. CUSTOM CAKES CTA (full-width banner with form/CTA)

10. TESTIMONIALS (carousel with customer photos)

11. ABOUT / STORY (image + text section)

12. OFFERS BANNER

13. FAQ ACCORDION

14. WHATSAPP FLOATING BUTTON (always visible)

15. FOOTER (dark background, elegant)
    About | Quick Links | Categories | Contact | Social | Newsletter
```

### Product Card Redesign
```
┌────────────────────────┐
│ [Product Photo]        │  ← Clean, professional photo
│ (hover: second angle)  │  ← Image swap on hover
│                        │
│ ───────────────────── │
│ Product Name           │  ← Serif font, elegant
│ Category               │  ← Small, muted
│ ₹XXX                  │  ← Bold gold price
│ [Add to Cart]          │  ← Minimal, appears on hover
│ 🌿 Eggless            │  ← Small badge
└────────────────────────┘
```

### Mobile-First Considerations
- Bottom nav: Home | Menu | Cart | Account
- Swipeable carousels for all horizontal sections
- Sticky header with cart count badge
- Product cards: 2 columns, compact
- Quick-add bottom sheet (not full page redirect)
- Smooth page transitions (Framer Motion or CSS)
